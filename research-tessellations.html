Geometric Structure of High-Dimensional Data

Overview

Understanding the intrinsic geometric properties of data in high dimensions is crucial for machine learning. I study how tools from convex geometry and stochastic geometry can characterize the structure of real-world datasets and inform algorithm design.

Key Questions

  • How does the geometry of data affect machine learning algorithm performance?
  • What can random projection theory tell us about dimensionality reduction?
  • How do concentration phenomena manifest in real data?
  • What is the typical structure of convex hulls of high-dimensional point clouds?

Main Results

Random Projections and Dimension Reduction

We proved new concentration bounds for random projections that improve upon classical Johnson-Lindenstrauss results under realistic data assumptions. These results provide theoretical guarantees for dimensionality reduction methods used in practice.

Convex Hulls of Random Point Sets

We characterized the typical number of faces and volume of convex hulls formed by random points in high dimensions, with applications to support vector machines and computational geometry.
