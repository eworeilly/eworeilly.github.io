About

I am an applied mathematician working at the intersection of geometry, probability, and data science. My research focuses on applications of stochastic geometry, geometry of convex bodies, point processes, and high-dimensional probability to problems in machine learning and data science.

I am particularly interested in understanding the geometric structure of high-dimensional data and developing mathematical foundations for modern machine learning algorithms.
