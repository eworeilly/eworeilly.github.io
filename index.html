About

I am an assistant professor at Johns Hopkins University. I am affiliated with the DSAI and MINDS institutes. My research lies broadly in the mathematics of data science and focuses on applications of point processes and stochastic geometry, geometry of convex bodies, and high-dimensional probability to complex data analysis.
